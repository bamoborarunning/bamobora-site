BAMOBORA® · Collection #001 Read the manifesto

We are BAMOBORA. A Portuguese activewear brand, and a collective of misfits grinding creativity. Not athletes. Still racing. Running is never the objective. It is the medium through which we experience creativity, cities and each other. Showing up is louder than finishing first. Then we challenge ourselves: a new distance, a faster pace, a harder route. A game against ourselves, never a ranking. We mix performance with tradition. Technical fabric on centuries-old streets. A bica after the run, plastic chairs in the sun, the café owner who knows us by name. Luxury isn't expensive. It's taking the time. We take movement where it doesn't belong. Into museums, markets and libraries, onto runways. Every event is a cultural intervention: never the same twice, announced once. Rooted in Portugal. Crafted for the world.

Yard Takeovers

Yard Takeovers are by invitation only. One night or day, one place, never the same twice. Part run, part runway, part party: the moments where you can grab a piece of art and culture, and the BAMOBORA world comes to life.

How to get invited: run with the Training Division
Training Division

Hear first

BAMOBORA® was born in 2026, somewhere between Lisbon and Braga. It started as a run. It became a way of seeing things.

We are a collective of misfits, the unfit and the restless. People who run, paint, shoot, design, play records and make things nobody asked for. We don’t run to be fast. We run into things. A gallery, a party, the city at 6am, a version of ourselves we hadn’t met yet.

Now we bring performance into the game. Not to win. To challenge ourselves, and to see what happens when sport and culture stop being two separate things.
