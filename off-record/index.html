Off Record · Yard Takeovers Nº 001 14.11 · 10:30 · Braga

Off Record. Every culture that ever mattered started somewhere no one was supposed to be. A basement. An empty warehouse. A street at four in the morning, in the rain, with people who never asked permission.

No one gave them a plan. No one told them the rules. They showed up, and that was enough.

Running forgot this. It became training plans, paces, apps, watches telling us who we are. Everything measured. Everything predicted. Everything safe.

We don’t believe in safe.

We believe running is another state of art. That the best thing that can happen is the thing nobody planned. That the city is most honest when it’s wet, dark and empty.

We won’t tell you what happens. You’ll find out when it’s too late to prepare. Bring the ones who would never leave you behind.

Running into things. Welcome to the crew.

The rules02

Before the start

  • 01

    Check-in

    From 10:30 to 10:45, at the meeting point. Crews are drafted there and we say the code that opens these rules. Briefing and Q&A at 10:45.

  • 02

    Crews

    25 runners, 5 crews of 5. Crews are made at check-in. You don’t choose yours.

  • 03

    One phone per runner

    Every runner records their own activity on Strava, on their own phone, carried by them from start to finish.

  • 04

    The start

    At 11:00 all crews start together from the meeting point, on the signal. Press Record on Strava at the signal, not before.

The run

  • 05

    Run anywhere

    There is no course. Any public street, square or path in Braga. Your crew decides the route together.

  • 06

    Stay together

    Your crew leaves together and finishes together. Nobody runs alone. The gap between your fastest and slowest runner costs points.

  • 07

    Never twice

    Your crew can’t run the same stretch of street twice. A stretch is the street between two junctions. Crossing your own route at a junction is allowed. The last 200 m back into the finish don’t count.

  • 08

    5.00 km minimum

    Every runner records at least 5.00 km. You can run more, but the clock keeps running.

  • 09

    Safety first

    Respect traffic lights and traffic. We count Strava’s moving time, so stopping at a red light costs you nothing.

  • 10

    The finish

    The finish is the meeting point. Stop Strava when you arrive.

After the run

  • 11

    Your entry

    Save the activity with your crew’s name and post it to the BAMOBORA club on Strava before you leave. No post, no time.

  • 12

    Results

    The jury checks every map, calculates the points and announces the ranking at breakfast, 11:45. The jury’s decision is final.

Points03
  • A

    Speed

    The average Strava moving time of your 5 runners. The fastest crew takes 60. The others take 60 × (best average ÷ your average).

    60
  • B

    Consistency

    The gap between the fastest and slowest runner in your crew. The smallest gap takes 25. The others take 25 × (smallest gap ÷ your gap).

    25
  • C

    Exploration

    The parishes (freguesias) of Braga your crew ran through, by the official map. A parish counts if your route enters it. The crew with the most takes 15. The others take 15 × (yours ÷ the most).

    15
